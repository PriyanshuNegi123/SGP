Shri Giraj Ji Polymers (SGP) is a prominent Anti-Static (ESD) Bags Manufacturer in India, offering premium-quality Electrostatic Discharge packaging solutions for electronics, automotive, semiconductor, electrical, aerospace and engineering industries. Located in Faridabad, Haryana, we manufacture high-performance Anti-Static Bags, ESD Poly Bags and Static Dissipative Packaging Bags designed to protect sensitive electronic components from static electricity, dust, moisture and physical damage during storage and transportation.

Manufactured using specially formulated anti-static materials, our ESD Bags effectively dissipate electrostatic charges and help prevent damage caused by electrostatic discharge. These static shielding bags are ideal for safeguarding printed circuit boards, integrated circuits, electronic assemblies, sensors, connectors and precision components.

As a trusted ESD Packaging Manufacturer, SGP supplies customized Anti-Static Packaging Solutions to OEMs, electronics manufacturers, exporters and industrial customers across India. Our Anti-Static Poly Bags are available in customized sizes, thicknesses, colours and printed formats to meet specific customer requirements.

With superior mechanical strength and dependable electrostatic protection, SGP provides reliable packaging solutions for industries where component safety is critical.
